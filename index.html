About PharmaLook

Healthcare access, made quick and simple.

At PharmaLook, we believe finding medicine shouldn't mean pharmacy-hopping. We built a platform that lets you locate prescription and over-the-counter medicines at nearby pharmacies in real time — whether you're at home, on the road, or caring for a loved one.

Our mission is to connect people with pharmacies instantly, with full transparency on pricing, availability, and location — for patients, caregivers, and anyone staying on top of their health.

FAQ

Common questions

 

How do I search for medicines?

Simply type the name of the medicine in the search bar to see which pharmacies have it in stock.

How do I know the medicine is available at the pharmacy?

PharmaLook shows real-time information on medicine availability from local, registered pharmacies.

Can I compare prices for the same medicine?

Yes — search results show prices from every pharmacy that has the medicine in stock, so you can compare.
